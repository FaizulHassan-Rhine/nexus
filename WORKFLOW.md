# Nexus — What This Platform Does

Nexus is a national digital hub for Bangladesh. It brings students, teachers, faculty, researchers, educational institutions, companies, training providers, and regulators such as UGC onto one platform so they can find and exchange opportunities.

The idea is simple: a learner from any recognized institution should be able to discover a course, internship, job, scholarship, or research opportunity that fits them — and an employer or institution should be able to reach verified people without relying only on informal networks.

## What people get

- A verified identity (National ID, birth certificate, or passport, depending on age and account type)
- A profile that describes skills, education, languages, interests, and availability
- A match score that explains *why* an opportunity fits — and what is missing
- A marketplace of jobs, internships, courses, scholarships, research calls, and partnerships
- Institutional review before most applications reach an employer
- Shared internship funding (50/50) between eligible companies and UGC
- Support during regular studies *and* during summer, winter, and semester breaks

---

## How the whole system works

1. **A person joins** and chooses their role (student, teacher, faculty, researcher, institution, company, training provider, or industry professional).
2. **They prove who they are.** Students also select their institution type so later fields match their reality (class/stream for school, diploma year for polytechnic, programme/department for university, and so on).
3. **They complete a profile** — skills, languages, career interests, documents.
4. **Opportunities appear** from companies, training providers, institutions, and research partners: local jobs in Bangladesh, international remote roles, language courses, bootcamps, workshops, internships, scholarships, and research collaboration.
5. **Nexus suggests matches** based on skills, education, institution type, language, experience, and interests. The person can see the score and the gaps.
6. **The learner applies.** For most internships and similar roles, the educational institution reviews the application first.
7. **If approved, it goes to the organization.** Shortlisting, interview, offer, and completion are tracked in one place.
8. **Where UGC co-funding applies**, stipend cost is shared and payments follow verified milestones.
9. **If something goes wrong**, helpdesk and dispute processes exist, with escalation to the institution and UGC.

Nothing is auto-placed. Matching recommends. People and institutions decide.

## Workflow by user

### Student
Register → choose institution type → submit identity document → build profile → discover matches (local jobs, remote international jobs, courses, scholarships) → apply → institution reviews → organization responds → track offer and completion → keep learning during vacations and semester breaks.

### Teacher
Register with school/college/madrasa → verify identity → add subjects and availability → support students with courses and opportunities → join workshops and collaboration.

### Faculty / Researcher
Register with institution → add research and teaching profile → find grants, exchange, consultancy, joint research, or technology transfer → apply or respond → manage the project.

### Company / Training provider
Register the organization → wait for verification → post jobs, internships, courses, or research calls → receive institution-reviewed candidates → hire or enroll → request co-funding if eligible → monitor progress.

### Institution administrator
Register the institution → get approved → review student and faculty applications → verify profiles → watch internships and partnerships → report results.

### UGC
Access is provisioned, not self-registered → oversee funding programmes → approve co-funding → resolve escalated disputes → see national activity.

### Helpdesk
Receive tickets → support users within the service target → escalate serious cases to institutions or UGC.

## What can be exchanged on Nexus

- Education and short-term learning (including language courses)
- Skills development, workshops, bootcamps, and certifications
- Internships, apprenticeships, and campus jobs
- Full-time, part-time, and international remote jobs
- Scholarships and student project funding
- Research collaboration, grants, and faculty exchange
- Industry–academia partnerships and technology transfer

## In one sentence

Nexus is a trusted meeting place where Bangladesh’s learners, educators, institutions, industry, and regulators can find each other and move opportunities from discovery to completion with verification and human review at every important step.
